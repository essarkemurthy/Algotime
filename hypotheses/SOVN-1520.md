# Hypothesis SOVN-1520 — NIFTY short ATM straddle, overnight only

Status: DRAFT, PARKED (cannot be run or traded on the current engine or account; recorded so the idea
is not re-invented and so its data is not touched by accident)

Family: NIFTY overnight short premium. Variants tried in family: 0.

## Economic reason
The same two papers as LSTR-0920 (Bhat, Pandey & Nageswara Rao 2024 on NIFTY; Muravyev & Ni 2020 on
SPX) find that the variance risk premium earned by option sellers is mainly compensation for
**overnight** risk: short option returns are positive and significant from close to open and negative
during the day. A seller who is short only overnight collects the part of the premium that is actually
paid for a risk (gaps while the market is closed) and avoids the intraday part that is given back. The
other side is the overnight hedger and the intraday buyer who roll into the close.

## Exact rules
- Instrument: NIFTY 50 weekly options, nearest expiry with DTE ≥ 1 (same calendar as LSTR-0920).
- Entry: sell 1 lot ATM call and 1 lot ATM put at the **15:20 bar open**, strike nearest 50 to spot at 15:20.
- Exit: buy both back at the **09:20 bar open** next trading day. No overnight adjustment is possible.
- R for the night = (entry straddle value − exit straddle value − costs) / entry straddle value.
- Skip rules: none in the backtest; event nights (budget, RBI, US CPI/FOMC) are slices.

## Why it is parked
- Margin: a short NIFTY straddle needs ≈ ₹1.6-1.9 lakh per lot (≈ ₹2.0-2.3 lakh into an expiry day)
  under current SPAN + exposure + ELM rules (broker guides, 2026). Usable capital today is ₹7,900.
- Engine: the paper engine and the live bridge are long-only (buy, then sell). Short premium needs
  margin tracking, a buy-to-close path and overnight position persistence, none of which exist.
- Risk: the premium is compensation for gap risk; a single 3% overnight gap on a 1-lot ATM straddle is a
  loss of roughly ₹35,000-50,000 at today's levels with no stop possible. A hedged form (iron fly with
  wings 300-400 points away) cuts margin to ≈ ₹30-40k and caps the loss, at the price of most of the
  premium; whether anything is left after costs is unknown and is a separate hypothesis.

## Data, gates
Identical data source as LSTR-0920 (the same contracts, 15:20 and next-day 09:20 bars), so the two
hypotheses share one fetch. Running both on the same fetched data is allowed because they are different
families with different sides and holding periods; neither may be used to re-tune the other. Gates as in
LSTR-0920 with mean R ≥ 0.02 and CI lower bound > 0, plus a gap-stress gate: the worst 5 nights' total
loss must not exceed 20 R.
